## Interactions
Select a clip, open the Filters tab, search or pick a category, click a look card — it applies to the clip at once and the preview updates live. Drag the intensity slider to blend it. Import a .cube file to add a custom look (stored on the clip).

## States
No clip selected: the panel asks to select one. No look applied: the grid shows all 30 looks, none highlighted. A broken .cube import shows a readable error and applies nothing.

## Result
The look is stored on the clip (`clip.lut`: name, size-8 data, intensity, source builtin/imported), undoable and autosaved. The `.cube` text for every built-in look is on disk in `assets/luts/` and round-trips through the parser.

## Edge cases
Applying a second look replaces the first (one LUT per clip). Intensity 0 leaves the frame unchanged. Imported .cube files keep their title as the look name.
