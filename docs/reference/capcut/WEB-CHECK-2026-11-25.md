(no sign-in prompt appeared). New empty project; one existing library clip (1:01) used as

## 1. Preview top-bar tools (clip selected on timeline)

- Tools sit in a **floating white pill centred above the preview**, plus a **Ratio** button
  at the preview's top-left, and vertical tabs (Basic, Background, Smart tools, Audio,
  Animation, Speed) at the preview's right edge.
- **Edit with AI** (sparkle icon, first in pill): opens a prompt bar at the bottom of the
  preview — "+ [clip thumbnail] 0:00 - 0:30 Describe your edit" — tooltip: "Drag either
  end of the clip to select the part you want to edit". X closes the bar.
- Icon "Add overlay" (frame with +): clicked — no panel opened.
- Icon "Fill": clicked — no panel; toggles preview fit/fill in place.
- Icon "Crop": opens a centred **Crop dialog** over the preview — Aspect ratio dropdown
  "Custom"; Mode "Manual crop" / "Auto reframe" (Pro diamond); Advanced settings with a
  Rotate slider at 0°; Reset and Apply buttons; Close X — plus crop handles (white
  circles) on the preview frame.
- Icon "Overlay" with chevron: small white dropdown below the pill, 4 icon-only buttons
  (no text labels).
- "…" (far right of pill): menu — Copy (Ctrl+C), Cut (Ctrl+X), Paste (Ctrl+V, greyed out),
  Duplicate (Ctrl+D), Delete (Backspace), Replace, Crop, Flip >, Overlay >, Add overlay.
- **Smart tools** (right-edge tab): panel slides in over the preview's right edge —
  Auto reframe (Pro), Retouch, Remove background (Pro), Camera tracking (Pro), Relight,
  AI movement, Optical flow. Auto reframe opens the same Crop dialog with "Auto reframe"
  selected ("Main objects will be recognized and kept in the frame", Image stabilization
  "Normal", Camera moving speed "Normal", "Analyzing your video…", Apply with Pro badge).
  So Auto reframe is a mode inside Crop, also reachable from Smart tools.
- **CC: NOT visible** — no CC button above/around the preview. Captions exist only as a
  "Captions" item in the left rail. (Desktop evidence for CC still to be checked against
  the 30 screenshots before changing Vidora's preview toolbar.)

## 2. Body effects

- Effects has two sub-tabs: **Video effects** and **Body effects**.
- Body effects rows (each with "View all"): Favorites (Glowing Doubles, Totem Flames,
  Lightning Bound, Cross Shift), Trending, Pro, Hits, Clone, Glowing lines, Stroke,
  Hallucination, Superpowers, Role (only "Unfair"), Mood.
- No on-screen label says the effect tracks a body region; tiles/names present them as
  person-centred — thumbnails show the effect on the person's body/face only (e.g. Neon
  Figure / Sketch Border outline just the figure, Face Glitch on the face, Clone rows show
  copies of the figure), unlike Video effects which act on the whole frame.
- Apply: **single click on a tile did NOT apply**; hovering shows **no + button and no
  tooltip — there is no + button on the tiles**. **Dragging a tile onto the timeline at
  the playhead added a small segment/marker on a track above the clip.**
- NOTE: the user reported (desktop) that a "+" option also applies effects. Web has no
  + button. Desktop wins conflicts per project rule — the "+" stays in Vidora until
  desktop evidence says otherwise.

## 3. Filters tab "Tools" group — REAL

- The Filters panel is one scrolling list of category rows. Between **Retro** and
  **Alternative** there is a **Tools** row with exactly 7 items: Enhance, 4K, HD Upscale,
  Quality Restoration, Night-view Color, HD Cam 2, HD Pet (HD Pet also appears in the Pet
  category). Laid out as a tile row like the filter categories, but they are **AI
  enhancement tools, not colour filters**.
- Clicking one opened no dialog — it applied directly: a small pill/marker appeared on a
  track above the clip at the playhead and the preview changed (control test: Mono "BW 1"
  turned preview black-and-white; HD Cam 2 made it high-contrast with blue edges).
- Selecting a marker opens the right-hand Basic panel showing **only an Intensity slider
  (100)**. Some Tools tiles carry a purple-diamond Pro badge (e.g. 4K); clicking them did
  not open a paywall in this session.

## 5. Tiles and applying

- Tiles in Effects, Filters and Transitions are all **square ~70×70 photo thumbnails**
  (a still/model image showing the look) with a short name label underneath, truncated
  (e.g. "Glowing D…"); some carry a purple-diamond Pro badge at top-left; while loading
  they show as pale grey blank squares. **No letters/gradients and no + button on any
  tile.** (Thumbnails load slowly — many grey placeholders for ~10 seconds; one Filters
  view briefly showed thumbnail load failures but the panel stayed usable.)
- **Effects (Video and Body): click does not apply; drag-and-drop onto the timeline clip
  does.** Clean test (Video effects > Trending > "Swing"): click and double-click did
  nothing; dragging the tile onto the timeline clip added a small purple/blue segment on
  the track above the clip at the playhead (00:30). Clicking the segment selected it (teal
  outline); the right rail showed only "Basic", and the Basic panel showed only a
  collapsed "Adjustments" header (no sliders expanded). The preview at the playhead
  showed no visible change. Deleted with Backspace.
- **Filters (including Tools): single click applies** (marker at the playhead + visible
  preview change).
- **Transitions: click does not apply** — with a single clip a toast appears:
  "Transitions can only be added between 2 consecutive clips"; the panel banner says
